Use this checklist before publishing a new item or updating an existing one.

## Check the content

- The title tells readers what the update is about.
- Names, dates, times, addresses, and contact details have been confirmed.
- The overview makes sense on its own and matches the full story.
- Old event dates and instructions have been removed from reused content.
- Paragraphs and lists are easy to read, and links go to the intended pages.

## Check the images and address

- The cover or body photos are appropriate and the pantry has permission to use them.
- Important subjects remain visible in the relevant crops.
- Meaningful body images have **Alt text**; captions are accurate where used.
- A new document has a clear, valid slug. An existing slug stays the same unless you intended to change the address and arranged updated links or redirects.
- A custom Page does not conflict with a fixed website route.

## Check publishing

- All required fields are complete and validation errors are resolved.
- The draft has finished saving.
- **Publish Date** is correct for post ordering; a future date is not being used as a substitute for scheduling.
- Referenced documents are published where needed.
- Preview has been checked in the **Drafts** perspective, including the homepage's latest-update section when affected.

## Check the public result

After publishing, open the website outside draft preview. Check the individual post/page, links, and any affected shared areas. For a new latest post, check `/posts` and the homepage's latest-update section. For Settings changes, check the footer or sharing-image result as appropriate.

If something is wrong, edit the document, review, and publish the correction. For persistent problems, see [Troubleshooting and support](troubleshooting.md).
